About UPYA

We believe youth are not waiting for change. They are already making it.

UPYA is a youth-led association serving young people through mentorship, practical skills, innovation and community service.

Our direction

Build confident, capable and connected young leaders.

Mission

To empower, equip, mentor and nurture tomorrow's world.

Vision

A generation of empowered youth transforming communities into centres of lasting positive change.

Our values

Teamwork

We make progress by working with one another.

Integrity

We act honestly and follow through on commitments.

Equity

Every young person deserves meaningful opportunity.

Accountability

We measure our work and learn from the results.
